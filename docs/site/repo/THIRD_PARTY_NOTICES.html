    Pixelarticons

    crates/horizon-theme/icons/pixel/*.svg are icons from Pixelarticons by Gerrit Halfmann, under the MIT license (crates/horizon-theme/icons/pixel/LICENSE-pixelarticons.txt). star.svg is Rusty Minecraft's own, drawn on the same grid.

    AMD FidelityFX SDK (FSR 3.1 frame generation)

    crates/horizon-client-render/src/gfx/frame_gen/optical_flow.wgsl is a port to WGSL of the optical flow shaders of the AMD FidelityFX SDK 1.1.4 (sdk/include/FidelityFX/gpu/opticalflow, at commit c6efa6bf7f20), and optical_flow.rs follows the SDK's sdk/src/components/opticalflow/ffx_opticalflow.cpp (its passes, their order and their dispatch sizes). Copyright (C) 2024 Advanced Micro Devices, Inc., under the MIT license, whose text heads both files. AMD and FidelityFX are AMD's trademarks; Rusty Minecraft is not affiliated with AMD.

    Mod loader logos

    The launcher shows the logo of the mod loader an instance plays with.

    • launcher/assets/logos/quilt.png is the Quilt mini icon (brand/128png/quilt_mini_icon_transparent.png) from QuiltMC/art, by the Quilt Project, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
    • launcher/assets/logos/neoforge.png is NeoForged's icon (art/neoforged_icon_16x16.png) from neoforged/.github, © the NeoForged team, created by Ridanisaurus, under CC BY 4.0. It is unmodified (the launcher scales it up by whole pixels).
    • Fabric's and Forge's icons are not shipped: neither publishes a license for its logo. The launcher fetches them from their own sites when an instance needs them (fabric-loader's icon, files.minecraftforge.net's).

    Each logo belongs to its project. Showing it says which loader an instance runs; it does not mean the project endorses Rusty Minecraft.

    Sign in with Microsoft

    The launcher's sign-in buttons follow Microsoft's published branding guidelines for apps that sign in with Microsoft accounts (Sign in with Microsoft branding). The four-square Microsoft symbol in launcher/src/microsoft.rs reproduces the squares, positions and colours of Microsoft's official ms-symbollockup_mssymbol_19.svg from that page, unmodified. Microsoft and its logo are trademarks of the Microsoft group of companies; Rusty Minecraft is not endorsed by Microsoft.
