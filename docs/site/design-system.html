    Rusty Minecraft's design system

    One design language for the launcher, the website and the few overlays the game draws that vanilla has no screen for (the on-screen keyboard a controller types with). Every game screen and the HUD are not part of it: they are vanilla's, because parity is the point of the game, and Rusty Minecraft's own settings are pages built from vanilla's widgets, the way mods add theirs.

    The tokens live in one place, crates/horizon-theme. The launcher (launcher/src/theme.rs) and the game (crates/horizon-client/src/ui_theme.rs) read them as Rust constants, and the website reads them as CSS custom properties generated from the same crate (cargo run -p xtask -- tokens writes crates/horizon-theme/tokens.css, which the website's build reads from main; a test fails when the file is out of date). Change a value in the crate and all three follow.

    Where it comes from

    The direction was set by mkdir.directory, a directory site that is almost nothing but type. What we take from it is its restraint rather than its content:

    1. Type is the interface. One family, Geist, and its mono. Large, tight display type (medium weight, -2 % tracking) carries the hierarchy, so there is no need for boxes, icons or colour to say what matters. Instrument Serif is gone.
    2. Lists, not cards. Content is a numbered list: a small mono index on the left (01, 02…), the name large, a mono detail on the right, and a 1 px rule between rows. Versions, settings, worlds, servers, release notes and the parity table are all this one component.
    3. One accent, used once. Neutrals do the work. The violet marks the single main action on a screen (Play, Download), focus, links and progress; selection is shown in ink, not in colour.
    4. Space instead of borders. Generous margins (64 px on wide screens, 16 px on phones), no shadows, no gradients, no outlines around groups. A surface tint appears only under the pointer or behind something that must stand apart (a console, a dialog).
    5. Quiet motion. Things arrive with a short fade and an 8 px rise, a few tens of milliseconds apart, eased out, never bouncy. Hovers fade a soft plate in behind the row.
    6. A signature. A giant, faint wordmark: at the foot of the website, behind the launcher's opening and the game's title screen.

    Tokens

    Colour

    Cool, slightly blue neutrals in two appearances with the same restraint, and Rusty Minecraft's violet. Every text colour passes WCAG AA (4.5:1) on every background it is used on; the tests in horizon-theme check each pair in both palettes.

    Token Light Dark Use
    page #fafafc #0d0e13 the background
    plate_soft #f3f3f8 #181a22 hover plate, fields, segmented track, console
    plate #ebecf4 #20222c pressed plate, switch off, the faint wordmark
    surface #ffffff #16181f dialogs and menus that sit above the page
    rule #e2e3ea #24262f 1 px rules between rows, tracks
    ink #16181f #ecedf3 text, selection, slider fill, the inverted segment
    ink_soft #3b3e49 #c5c7d1 secondary text, values
    muted #646875 #8b8f9c labels, descriptions, mono indices and details
    accent #5b3fd6 #9a86ff the main action, links, focus, progress
    on_accent #ffffff #0d0e13 text on the accent
    danger #b4372a #f07a68 errors, the crash notice's dot
    success #1d7a48 #45c27a "Matches vanilla", "Ready"

    The game's menus draw over the live title backdrop, always in the dark palette, with the page colour as a flat veil so text keeps its contrast whatever the world shows: VEIL (0.90) on every page and in a world, HOME_VEIL (0.80) on the title's home page, which shows more of the world and keeps to ink and ink_soft. Muted text over the backdrop is BACKDROP_MUTED (#9fa3ae). A test holds every pair to AA over a backdrop of luminance 0.77 (the snowiest title panorama measured reaches 0.49), on the bare veil and on a selected row's plate. Their plates are the dark ink at 8 % and 14 % rather than plate_soft and plate: over the veil they come out the same, and they stay visible over a bright backdrop.

    Space, shape and lines

    • Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Page gutter 64 (apps and wide web), 16 on phones. Text measure at most 56 characters for paragraphs.
    • Radii: 8 for fields and small controls, 12 for plates (hover rows, console, dialogs), full for buttons, segmented controls and switches.
    • Lines: 1 px, rule. No other borders.
    • Heights: control 32, large control (Play) 40, segment 28, list row 64 (apps), 86 (web).

    Motion

    Token Duration Easing Use
    quick 150 ms cubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1) hovers, presses, colour
    move 240 ms cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1) selection sliding, switches, page changes
    enter 600 ms cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1) content arriving: opacity 0→1, rise 8 px
    stagger 40 ms delay between list rows arriving

    prefers-reduced-motion (web) turns enter into a plain fade.

    Components

    • Index list (the signature): micro mono index in muted (left, 40 px column), the name in title (or ui/label for dense lists such as settings), details in micro mono muted on the right, a rule under every row. Hover: a plate_soft plate with radius 12 fades in, extending 16 px past the row on each side, and the index turns ink.
    • Buttons: pill shaped, label size, medium weight, 32 high (40 for the one large action). Primary is accent with on_accent text, at most one per screen. Secondary is plate_soft with ink text. Ghost is muted text that turns ink with a plate_soft plate under the pointer. Pressed: plate (or the accent at 85 %). Disabled: 40 % opacity.
    • Fields: 36 high, plate_soft, radius 8, no border; focus draws a 1 px accent ring. Monospace for paths and commands.
    • Switch: 36×20 pill, plate when off, accent when on, a white 16 px knob that slides over move.
    • Slider: a 2 px rule track, the filled part ink, a 14 px ink knob. The value is written in mono beside the label, not on the track.
    • Segmented control / tabs: a plate_soft pill; the selected segment is an ink pill with page text that slides over move.
    • Navigation: text only. Launcher: a top bar with the mark and "Rusty Minecraft" on the left and the pages on the right, label size, muted, the current one ink on a soft pill. Game menus keep their sidebar layout (it suits a controller), but as a plain column of text items under the mark, with no panel tint and no border: the current one ink on a soft pill that slides to it.
    • Page header: display title, then one body line in muted, 56 characters at most.
    • Notices and dialogs: a plate_soft plate, radius 12, 16–24 padding, no border; a 6 px dot in danger (or accent) leads the title. Actions are buttons in a row below.
    • Progress: a 2 px rule track and an accent fill; indeterminate work shuttles a 30 % segment.
    • Console: plate_soft plate, radius 12, micro/caption mono in ink_soft, newest line at the bottom, the command field inside the plate under a rule.
    • Empty states: a short muted sentence and one secondary button, centred in the space.

    Surfaces

    • Launcher (launcher/): moving to the Night look below, screen by screen; screens not rebuilt yet draw the Blocks parts in the night's colours (Night::blocks). A top bar (wordmark, the pages as tabs, the account chip, Settings), a sidebar (the instances, the hosted servers, the launcher's own version and channel at its foot), and the page over a stone texture. Home opens on a pixel landscape banner with the hero, the version picker and Play; Mods holds the mod, resource pack and modpack tabs; Versions and Patch notes are lists of panels; Settings is grouped panels of rows. Dark by default, Light or System in Settings (Appearance).
    • Website (horizon-rust/horizon-site): the Blocks look below. The same page structure as before (what it is, screenshots, parity, launcher, patch notes, community), headings in Pixelify Sans, lists as stacks of panels, the stone texture behind the page and Rusty Minecraft's pixel landscape (a dawn or a sunset) behind the hero. Light and dark follow the system, with a toggle. A giant wordmark in pixels sits in the footer.
    • Game: vanilla's screens. The on-screen keyboard is a rounded plate of keys in the page colour, the focused key in the accent.

    Blocks

    A blocky, Minecraft-like look in Rusty Minecraft's purple, for the website and the redesigned launcher: Minecraft's inventory rather than a document. Its colours are horizon_theme::Blocks (BLOCKS_LIGHT, BLOCKS_DARK), which tokens.css carries under the same names as before plus --panel, --raised, --button, --well, --bevel, --lip, --outline and the --face of the main action. The game's menus keep LIGHT and DARK.

    • Pixel type for what names and what you act on. Headings, navigation, buttons and the wordmark use Pixelify Sans (OFL; Medium, SemiBold, Bold); paragraphs and numbers stay in Geist and Geist Mono so long text reads easily. The bundled copies add the three uppercase Cyrillic letters upstream leaves out, О, П and І: the Latin O's glyph, the lowercase п lifted to that weight's cap height, and the Latin I's glyph, so a Russian or Ukrainian heading stays in the pixel face.
    • Blocks, not rounded cards. Every surface is square: a 2 px dark outline, a 2 px light bevel along the top and a darker lip along the bottom. The main action is the violet face with its own bevel and a 4 px lip; it sinks a pixel when pressed. Code and fields are sunk wells.
    • Inventory tabs switch the kinds in Mods: the open tab joins the panel under it.
    • Slots hold pictures: a mod's or pack's own icon and gallery images from its source, downloaded when first shown and kept on disk (launcher/src/browser/images.rs); the launcher ships no third-party pictures. The Rusty Minecraft instance shows a grass block drawn from the player's own prepared Minecraft textures. Sources are named in text ("from Modrinth"): Modrinth's logo is all rights reserved, so it is not shown.
    • Partner banner: the one exception to shipping no third-party pictures. Sculk, a server host, sponsors Rusty Minecraft: its banner (launcher/assets/sculk-banner.webp, launcher/src/partner.rs) sits under a muted mono "Partner" label at the end of the Servers page and opens Sculk's page in the browser. The sidebar of the other pages shows a card made for its width instead: Sculk's characters and logo cropped from the same banner, and the text drawn by the launcher in its language, so it stays readable; it keeps Sculk's dark card, cyan and rounded corners in both themes, as an ad does, and turns compact (characters beside the text) when the window is too short. The website shows the banner the same way on Home and the hosting docs, as a rel="sponsored" link.
    • Pixel art drawn in code. The stone behind the pages is close shades of the page colour (TILE_SHADES), never Minecraft's stone; the landscape is a banded sky, a sun cut in slices like the mark, hills and square trees (a sunset in dark, a dawn in light). The launcher bakes them in launcher/src/art.rs at the window's scale so each texel covers whole device pixels; the website draws them in its scripts/art.mjs.
    • Icons: Pixelarticons (MIT), 24 unit pixel SVGs in crates/horizon-theme/icons/pixel/, tinted with the text colour.
    • Contrast: every text colour meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) on the panels, the page and the lightest stone shade in both palettes (blocks_text_meets_wcag_aa_in_both_appearances).

    Night

    The launcher's look from 0.3: a pixel night sky that turns to a sunset at the horizon, with the content on top. Its tokens are horizon_theme::night (NIGHT, and DAWN for players who pick Light); tokens.css carries them as --night-*. The game's menus don't use it.

    • The sky is the brand, the content is the UI. The sky, the stepped sun, the banded clouds and the skyline are pixel art drawn in code on one grid per layer, edges crisp, never smoothed. On top of it the screen is made of what the player owns: instance icons, world thumbnails, server icons, their skin's head, mods and counts. No ornaments, no empty cards.
    • Plates only where text needs them. A plate is card at its opacity with a 1 px stroke; card_hi for a selected or raised one. Text sits on the bare sky only over its upper stops (UNCARDED_STOPS): the sunset is too bright for light text. A plate cuts each corner in two 4 px steps (PIXEL_CORNER), like the art, and its stroke follows the steps. The main action cuts one step; other controls round at 8 (at 1x a step vanishes on a pale fill and looks notched on a thin ring: compared in the specimen).
    • One accent. accent fills the one main action (Play, Create, Install) and progress; accent_hi is the accent as text and the 2 px focus ring. Everything else is ink on plates.
    • Bricolage Grotesque (SIL OFL 1.1, fonts/OFL-BricolageGrotesque.txt), cut from upstream's variable font by tools/bake_bricolage.py: the UI family at optical size 14 (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold) and Bricolage Grotesque Display (Bold at optical size 72) for instance and page names. Geist Mono stays for paths, codes and counts. Upstream has no Cyrillic, so the bake merges Geist's (OFL, fonts/OFL-Geist.txt) into every cut: the letters drawn like Latin ones reuse Bricolage's, the others are Geist's scaled to Bricolage's cap and x-heights at a matching weight.
    • Two or three sizes per screen, from night::TYPE:
    Token App px Line Tracking (web) Weight Use
    night-display 56 1.0 -0.035em 700 instance and page names (display cut)
    night-title 20 1.25 -0.01em 700 section and card heads
    night-ui 14 1.45 -0.005em 500 text, rows, buttons, navigation
    night-caption 12 1.4 0 400 one-line states and details
    • Colour (night / dawn). Every text colour reaches AA on both plates over every stop of the sky, and on the upper stops, the page and the sidebar (night_text_meets_wcag_aa_over_the_sky).

    • Motion that means something: the sky drifts slowly and stops when the window is hidden or unfocused; the interface moves only to show cause and effect (a row arrives where it was added, progress fills). Reduced motion: nothing moves. The launcher's sky (launcher/src/sky.rs) moves in whole texels on a half-second beat, so it asks for two frames a second at most; HORIZON_LAUNCHER_MOTION=off stills it, always keeps it moving unfocused (for recordings).
    • Copy says what happens ("Downloads 26.3 and Java 21, about 600 MB"), never praises.
    • The specimen (HORIZON_LAUNCHER_PAGE=specimen horizon-launcher) draws the type and colours in the launcher itself.
    • The frame (launcher/src/shell.rs): the sidebar is one plate from the window's top to its bottom (the wordmark, instances by group, servers, the launcher's version); the top bar over the pages has no plate, only a rule. A selected row or tab sits on card_hi cut in steps (two for rows, one for tabs and icon buttons); a hovered one gets the same shape at half its opacity, eased in. A row's second line is its state, after a small square in the state's colour.
    • Home (launcher/src/home.rs): the selected instance's name in the display cut over the bare sky, a caption above it naming what it plays and its state (the same square), and when it was played below. Play is the main action (night::PLAY tall, cut in one step); the version picker is a raised control of the same height; Mods and Folder are quiet buttons. A Rusty Minecraft instance gets a plate on the right: a release's notes in one line (not a Nightly's) and Join a friend. While it gets ready, Play names it ("Getting ready… 34%") beside Cancel, and below sit the bar, what is happening with the time left, and the steps (game files, Java, sounds, prepare, play), the current one lit, those done ticked: steps, never the log (HORIZON_LAUNCHER_PAGE=getting-ready shows it for screenshots, nothing runs).
    • Instance icons (launcher/src/instance_icons.rs): the sidebar's rows, Home's hero (beside the name) and the instance dialog show the same icon. Its own picture first (icon.png in its game folder: one the player chose with Choose image…, scaled to 128 pixels, or its modpack's icon from where it was installed), else the block or colour chosen in the dialog, else its mark: the loader's logo (Fabric, Quilt, Forge, NeoForge), the grass block for plain Minecraft, Rusty Minecraft's for Rusty Minecraft. The letter tile is the last resort. The New instance dialog's loader cards show the same logos. Pixel-art logos are scaled by whole pixels; each is decoded once per size.
    • The instance's tabs (launcher/src/home_tabs.rs): under Home's hero, one tab at a time instead of one long page: Overview (what Play needs: the instance, the game or its resources, the account), Worlds, Multiplayer, Packs, then Screenshots and Console once they have something. A tab names how many it holds ("Worlds 3"); its own buttons (Add server, Open folder) sit at the strip's end, where a list's header would put them (HORIZON_LAUNCHER_PAGE=worlds, packs... opens one for screenshots).
    • The Mods tab (launcher/src/instance_mods.rs): the instance's mods on one nearly opaque card, its loader in the header ("Fabric mods", "Rusty Minecraft mods") beside a filter. A row is the mod's own icon (Modrinth's, else its jar's, else a glyph), its name and version, where it comes from (Modrinth, "needed by another mod", or a local file by name), an update as an amber pill when the browser found one, and a switch (a Java jar turned off is renamed .disabled). Update all and Add mods sit at the strip's end (HORIZON_LAUNCHER_PAGE=instance-mods).
    • Sign in with Microsoft (launcher/src/microsoft.rs): every button that signs in is Microsoft's own, drawn as Microsoft publishes it: 41 pixels tall, its four-square symbol, the text semibold at 15 pixels, #2F2F2F with white text at night, white with #5E5E5E text and a #8C8C8C border at dawn, square corners. Not one of the launcher's cut plates: Microsoft's guidelines ask for the button as it is.
    • The sign-in sheet (launcher/src/owner_page.rs, the owner gate): an opaque plate over the dimmed launcher, never instead of it. It says why (the account that owns Java Edition, no install needed), the code in mono on a raised box with Copy, and Open link as the main action; the window goes on by itself once Microsoft answers (HORIZON_LAUNCHER_PAGE=sign-in shows it with a made-up code for screenshots).
    • New instance (launcher/src/new_instance.rs): an opaque plate over the dimmed launcher, tabs for the kind (Rusty Minecraft, Vanilla, Modded). A Java kind lists Mojang's versions (search, Snapshots, Old; the newest release chosen), Modded adds the loader (Fabric or Quilt, at its newest stable version), and the name follows the choice ("Fabric 1.21.4") until the player types one. Create is the main action.

    The mark

    Rusty Minecraft line. A sun half set below a horizon, drawn as three horizontal bars of 4, 8 and 10 units over a 12 unit line, on a 16 unit grid. The bars are the sun cut by the horizon into slices, which also reads as layers of terrain and as a nod to voxels without drawing a block. It is built on whole units of the 16 px grid so it stays sharp at 16 px, and it scales without detail to 1024 px.

    • Monochrome: the mark in ink on light, in ink (dark) on dark; the line can take the accent.
    • App icon: the mark in #ecedf3 with the line in #9a86ff, on a #16181f rounded square (macOS grid: 824 px tile with 185 px corners in a 1024 canvas; Windows and Linux use the full square with 22 % corners).
    • Exports and where they go: docs/media/brand/README.md.
